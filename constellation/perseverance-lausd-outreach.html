    Perseverance | LAUSD Outreach

    "One city, one heart" - Perseverance by Curiosity ⭐

    Perseverance is a program focused on the LAUSD region of FIRST Tech Challenge through the i.am Angel Foundation. Named after Curiosity's sister mars rover, the goal of this program is to provide support, mentorship, and resources to a typically underserved region. Below, we have some resources for the program, our events for this season, and anything else related to Perseverance that can be accessed by any teams.
