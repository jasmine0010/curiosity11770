    Stars | FTC Teams and Pairings

    Constellation is a program to help connect FTC teams across the globe to promote learning and community. Based on teams’ strengths and areas for improvement, we match them up with a team that has complementary skills to create a mutually beneficial partnership. Check out the interactive map we made of all of the current constellation pairings!

    Interested in joining Constellation? Fill out the Constellation Match Form to get started!

    Open FTC team pairings
    Constellation Match Form

    We strive to strengthen our existing connections while expanding our network to teams worldwide, fostering opportunities to learn from, communicate with, and teach one another. Through these efforts, we aim to build a sense of community and partnership among teams globally, enabling continuous collaboration and growth to uplift our competitors in FTC.
