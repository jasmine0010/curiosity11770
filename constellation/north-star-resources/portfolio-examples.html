    Portfolio Examples

    As a team that has received the Think Award at the World Championship, we’ve developed a portfolio that highlights our engineering design process, strategic decisions, and technical growth at the highest level of competition. These examples are meant to inspire other teams by showing how to communicate ideas clearly, document iteration, and use visuals effectively. We encourage teams to use them as a reference point, so you can create a portfolio that truly reflects your own unique journey and innovations. The best portfolio is the one your team can write!
