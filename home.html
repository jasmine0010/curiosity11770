Purpose

We are a robotics team of girls and gender minorities from Marlborough School in Los Angeles. We have participated in the FIRST Tech Challenge (FTC) since 2016. Our mission is to set an example for aspiring young scientists and demonstrate what young women can accomplish in the world of STEM.

Curiosity Cares

Team Curiosity is on a mission to recycle as many FTC game elements as possible by reclaiming their plastic and keeping them out of landfills.
