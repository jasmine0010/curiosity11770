About Us

Purpose

We are a robotics team of girls and gender minorities from Marlborough School in Los Angeles. We have participated in FIRST Tech Challenge (FTC) since 2016. Our mission is to set an example for aspiring young scientists and to demonstrate what young women can accomplish in the world of STEM.
