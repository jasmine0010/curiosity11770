Kickoff9/7/24

Curiosity traveled to Monrovia today for the Into the Deep Season kickoff! We had an amazing time seeing the new game, and we also presented two portfolio workshops to over 150 people. We also made an appearance in the game video (SO EXCITING!!!) and started considering strategy and early design ideas for this years' game in a three hour work session. While we were slightly sad there was no live fish in the game, the climbing during endgame and the clip/human player interaction seem exciting! We can't wait to dive into this season!

Back to school (and this blog)8/29/24

Hi Everyone! We know it's been a while (some may say a long while) but we are back and better than ever! We had a lot going on last season, including 12 new team members, but we are back to this blog! We've spent our offseason planning out outreach, including our FLL team, keeping up our connections with STEM professionals, portfolio feedbacks, and some new, extensive outreach as well. We've also started planning out our season and team structure. Keep an eye out for more updates!

03 /

Regional Championship3/11/23

Curiosity had an amazing time at regionals and are elated to announce our qualification for the World Championships in Houston! We went undefeated in our qualifying matches and entered alliance selection as the first seed. We were unfortunately eliminated in semifinals. Thank you to our alliance partners Pineapple Something 20824 and Alpha Genesis 6436! Despite our defeat in semifinals, we stayed hopeful and won 1st place Inspire Award as well as 2nd place Promote Award. We are thrilled to be advancing to World Championships along with two other Southern California teams, Blueprints 7767 and GatorBytes 18457!

Inter-league tournament2/26/23

ILT was a major success for Team Curiosity! We had a great time connecting with the rest of our league and competing for the six advancement slots. We went undefeated in our qualifying matches and were winning alliance captain. A special thank you to our alliance partners Alpha Genesis 6436 and Marlbots 3526! We also won 3rd place Inspire Award, 3rd place Think Award, and 1st place Connect Award.

Championships4/25/21

Yesterday, we competed in the LAFTC Championships! We were so proud of the progress we had made on the robot since the qualifying tournament. Our highest score was 201 points, a team record. We won first place Think, third place Connect, and second place Compass for our amazing mentor, Mr. Witman. Congratulations to all the teams who competed! We had so much fun and it was an incredible day.

12 /

Championship updates4/19/21

We're working hard preparing for Regionals on 4/24! Other than updating our engineering notebook, portfolio, and judging, we are making some upgrades to the robot. First, we added on "ring wands" to block the rings from getting past our bot in Tele-Op. This doubles as a driver controlled enhancement! Next, we increased the speed of our auto with the goal to be able to pick up a second wobble goal. We hope to get that done before next week's competition!
