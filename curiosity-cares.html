Curiosity Cares

01 /

Team Curiosity is on a mission to recycle as many FTC game elements as possible by reclaiming their plastic and keeping them out of landfills. We’re currently accepting donations of retired field elements from teams worldwide.

🚚 If you donate 40 or more elements, we’ll cover your shipping costs. Otherwise, we still gladly accept any game elements–just note that we won’t be able to cover shipping. If you have concerns about being able to afford shipping, please reach out either via Instagram DM or email. 

📍If you're located near Los Angeles [SoCal or LAUSD Region], we’re happy to drive out and pick them up–no matter the quantity.

If your team is interested in donating, please fill out the quick form below (takes less than 1 minute).
