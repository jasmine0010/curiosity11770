Engineering the Perfect Portfolio

Our essential portfolio presentation tips focus on helping teams create a compelling, polished, and authentic showcase of their season.

We emphasize clear organization, consistent design, and purposeful use of visuals to tell your engineering story effectively.

Open Curiosity portfolio presentation 2024.mp4

Key points include...

  • Highlighting your design process with supporting data/calculations
  • Minimizing wasted space
  • Using formatting techniques like selective bolding to guide the reader’s attention
  • Using templets and keeping the portfolio cohesive

Above all, your portfolio should reflect your team’s unique journey and personality–making it memorable both technically and personally. This presentation sets the foundation for building portfolios that stand out in judging and represent your hard work with confidence.
