2021–22 Season

OverviewRobotMeet the TeamHighlightsOutreach
01 /

Our sixth season of FTC has concluded, and we are so grateful to all of the mentors, referees, and volunteers who have guided us along the way. This season, we welcomed five new members and all members learned more about important robot-related skills such as CADing, coding, and hardware. In order to work towards our goal of truly embodying FIRST's mission of connecting with our community, we hosted multiple outreach events with organizations such as Kitten Rescue–Los Angeles, the Trevor Project, and Mar Vista Family Center. Overall, we had a wonderful sixth season! You can also look at a more in depth description of our outreach here, a description of our robot here, and meet this year's team here.
