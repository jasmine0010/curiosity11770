As a team, we’ve learned that small details can make a big difference when it comes to portfolio design. From using bolding to highlight your best work to keeping your layout clean and consistent, these tips help make your portfolio both professional and memorable. We’ve used all of them ourselves to better communicate our design process, team identity, and season highlights.

  1. Diagrams and visual aids are essential for communicating your robot’s design clearly and effectively. They make complex mechanisms more accessible and help judges and readers quickly grasp your engineering decisions. Check out this example for inspiration ➡️

2. Use selective bolding to draw attention to key details—especially the parts of your portfolio you’re most proud of. This helps judges quickly identify your team’s standout achievements.

3. Minimize white space strategically. With only a few pages to capture your entire season, it’s essential to use every inch effectively—without overwhelming the reader.

4. Maintain a consistent font, color scheme, and layout to create a polished and cohesive portfolio. Visual harmony reflects professionalism and thoughtful design.

5. Clearly explain how your design process led to your final solution. Supporting visuals like calculations, diagrams, and performance charts can make your process more compelling and easier to follow.

6. Let your team’s personality shine through. Your portfolio isn’t just technical—it’s a reflection of your values, spirit, and collaboration. Make it memorable. Check out our chart below!
